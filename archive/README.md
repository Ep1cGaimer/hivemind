# HiveMind archive

This folder contains the historical concept-generation and review record for HiveMind.

The numbered files are preserved in their original form so the reasoning, dissent,
scoring, attacks, repairs, and validation limits remain auditable. They are reference
material, not startup instructions for implementation.

Use the current product documents in the workspace root instead:

- [`HIVEMIND_IDEA.md`](../HIVEMIND_IDEA.md) — product brief and boundary
- [`HIVEMIND_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md`](../HIVEMIND_GRAPH_CONTRACT.md) — graph/data contract
- [`HIVEMIND_DECISION_MEMO.md`](../HIVEMIND_DECISION_MEMO.md) — conditional decision and gates
- [`HIVEMIND_POSITION_LEDGER.md`](../HIVEMIND_POSITION_LEDGER.md) — positions and dissent
- [`STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md) — current authorization and implementation status
- [`agent.md`](../agent.md) — working instructions for agents

Do not treat archived paper exercises as runtime evidence. The archive contains no
measured retention result, benchmark, demand validation, or approval to build.


The pre-Git workspace README, capability brief, and evidence graph contract are
preserved as `legacy-workspace-readme.md`, `legacy-capability-brief.md`, and
`legacy-evidence-graph-contract.md`. They describe the separate historical
Juniper fixture. Current product rules live in the root Git-aware brief/contract.
