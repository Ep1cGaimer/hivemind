# Labeled validation — company-agent memory, 2026-10-15

Primary official docs and research papers only. A paper abstract verifies the authors' stated method/claim, not independently replicated performance, production adoption or customer demand. No reported research percentages are our results. Initial product pains/ingestion economics remain hypotheses. No competitor absence inferred from incomplete documentation.

V1. Glean already describes a company knowledge graph connecting people/content/activity; permission-aware retrieval, identity alignment and multi-step agents; MCP access from external AI hosts. A unified company brain, permissions, connectors, durable access to company knowledge, or an MCP wrapper alone is not a differentiation claim.
https://docs.glean.com/connectors/connectors-power-glean
https://docs.glean.com/user-guide/mcp/usage
https://docs.glean.com/agents/how-agents-work

V2. Graphiti officially supports temporal memory, fact invalidation, custom entities, hybrid search and an MCP server, with a FalkorDB backend. A generic temporal company graph or memory MCP is already infrastructure. No untested SDK/query compatibility claimed.
https://github.com/getzep/graphiti

V3. Collaborative Memory (2025) explicitly models users/agents/resources under changing permissions, immutable memory provenance, filtered/transformed read views and governed memory writes. Read-time lineage/permission-aware derived memory is not a new research mechanism. Paper evidence is particularly close to the access-control concepts.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18279
https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18279v1

V4. Governed Shared Memory (2026) describes scoped retrieval, temporal supersession, provenance and governed propagation in a fleet-memory service; it reports architectural failures as well as results. These failure classes are relevant evidence but claimed numbers are not independently verified here. The broad governed shared-memory product space is populated.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24535

V5. MAP-Graph (2026) explicitly studies provenance-aware shared memory and whether relevant evidence is admissible for an agent/action. Provenance plus admission is not by itself new.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10509

V6. Beyond Memory Majority (2026) directly studies correlated upstream sources behind different agents' memories and false majority, proposing latent-source reasoning. This supports a source-echo failure mechanism but removes any claim that origin-aware arbitration is a new algorithm/research insight. A narrowly demonstrated company workflow and observable recorded lineage could still be a product hypothesis; do not call it novel without comparing stronger controls.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19701

V7. Hallucination Cascade (2026) studies claim-level error propagation through agent sequences. It supports investigating propagation, not that all company agents fail or that our graph solves hallucination generally.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07937

V8. Gong already advertises cross-sales/customer-success continuity of promises, agents capturing commitments/action items and owner/date follow-up drafts, and trackers detecting concepts/promotions/risks. Generic promise extraction, handoff summaries and follow-up are occupied. Exact scoped authority, acceptance coverage or derivation-root operations need a demonstrated differentiating job; page omissions do not prove absence.
https://www.gong.io/customer-success-software
https://help.gong.io/docs/agent-use-case-and-examples
https://help.gong.io/docs/about-to-dos
https://help.gong.io/docs/about-trackers

V9. OPA explicitly supports dynamic/external data and context-aware policy decisions, including input, pushed data and evaluation-time fetch. The assertion that OPA is only a static build-time linter is false and must be withdrawn. A synthetic organizational rule is not a legal/regulatory conclusion.
https://www.openpolicyagent.org/docs/external-data

Rules for all evaluations: same facts/relations/model/budget; no scripted wrong baseline or requirement that a baseline must miss 50–80%. A deterministic graph query tying SQL does not disqualify meaningful LLM interpretation, but a narrator over an already-computed answer fails. Shared company memory must demonstrate an actual later agent/session using earlier memory with a valid evidence path, not just more agents or a task queue.

V10. Productboard explicitly de-duplicates repeated feedback by the same user on a feature for Customer Importance Score, and links source passages to feature insights. Dovetail advertises source-traceable AI insights/customer evidence and cross-team research reuse. Therefore 'count distinct customers rather than repeated mentions' and 'cite original feedback' are occupied product capabilities. This does not by itself settle arbitrary multi-hop agent-derived lineage, but it is a direct challenger rather than a weak vector-only baseline.
https://support.productboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058215013-Use-the-Customer-Importance-Score-to-surface-your-top-requested-features
https://dovetail.com/solutions/research-repository/
https://dovetail.com/blog/how-customer-insights-saved-us-from-the-ai-echo-chamber/

V11. Truck-factor research explicitly studies concentration of software knowledge, proposes estimation methods and compares some estimates with developers' responses. This supports concentration as a real research topic, not inference that missing graph edges prove undocumented/sole knowledge. Capture-to-successor-memory workflow demand remains to validate.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.06766

V12. Gainsight's official materials explicitly describe sales-to-services handoff failures and standardized onboarding/handoff practices; Gong also publishes agent prompts for handoff risk and deal continuity. These are vendor descriptions of an existing job, not independent proof of our semantic acceptance gap or prevalence percentages. No quoted third-party survey percentages are adopted.
https://www.gainsight.com/blog/owning-customer-onboarding-gainsight-essentials/
https://www.gainsight.com/blog/aligning-your-sales-to-cs-handoff-in-the-age-of-ai/

V13 (added after initial scoring; must be included in direct repair/rebuttal). Several primary product pages advertise precisely targeted employee knowledge capture/handover, not merely wikis: Flamekeeper says it identifies ownership, captures institutional knowledge, resolves gaps and produces approved handovers; Implix describes decisions/exceptions tied to a departing person and targeted interviews; Culturro describes pulling documents/tickets/systems/relationships into an agent interview and manager/successor handover. These are verified advertised claims, not evidence of customer scale, implementation quality or equivalent exact graph operations. They directly weaken H03's differentiation: an unanswered coverage-driven question and successor memory cannot be claimed as absent from current products. Reject mere renaming, or test a precise workflow distinction honestly; no presumption that a graph makes an existing product new.
